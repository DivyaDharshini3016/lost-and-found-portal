Help & safety

Who can see my phone number or email?

Only logged-in users, and only after they tap “Show contact” on an approved report.

I found something. What should I do?

Report it here, and if you can, also hand it to the security office or admin desk. Never post full ID, card or account numbers.

When will my report appear?

After an admin approves it. Edits to a report are approved again too.

How do I meet the owner safely?

Meet in a busy public place such as the main gate or library desk. Ask them to describe something not in the post (colour, stickers, what’s inside) before handing it over.

I forgot my password.

Log out, then tap “Forgot password?” on the login page and answer your security question.

I got my item back. Now what?

Open Account, find your report and tap “Mark returned” so others stop searching.
